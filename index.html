Working on discourse and language understanding.

Hi, I'm Max. I currently work on discourse structure annotation and discourse-level modeling, with a particular focus on perspectivism and human label variation.

Research & Interests

My research interests lie in natural language processing, discourse analysis, and machine learning for language-focused research. More broadly, I am interested in how computational methods can support the study of language, annotation, and variation.

Current Focus

  • Discourse-level language understanding and annotation
  • Human label variation and disagreement in annotation
  • Perspectivist approaches to language understanding
